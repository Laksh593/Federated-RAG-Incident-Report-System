SECURITY PROTOCOL: FIGHTING / PHYSICAL ALTERCATION

1. IMMEDIATE RESPONSE
   - Upon detection of fighting or physical violence, the security operator must immediately notify local law enforcement.
   - Do not attempt to intervene physically unless necessary to prevent loss of life.
   - Use the PA system (if available) to issue a verbal warning to cease hostilities.

2. DOCUMENTATION
   - Log the exact start and end time of the altercation.
   - Preserve video footage of the incident and 5 minutes prior/post.
   - Note the number of individuals involved and descriptive features.

3. POST-INCIDENT
   - File a Level 1 Incident Report within 1 hour.
   - Medical assistance should be summoned immediately if injuries are apparent.
   - Isolate the area until law enforcement arrives to preserve evidence.

REF: SEC-POL-001-FIGHT
